Why did the transfer fail?
First test one slow unloaded move, then repeat with a heavier carton. A reachable position can still require excessive torque. Inspect the smallest Jacobian determinant, the rate peak and the first guard intersection. Try the other elbow branch in the robot cell and repeat.


The two rigid links rotate in a horizontal plane. The model uses uniform 2 kg and 1 kg links and a point payload, with inertia and Coriolis terms. Quintic joint interpolation starts and finishes at rest. The arm checks joint rates, torque, singularity and circular keep-out clearance every 20 ms. Belt tracking targets a predicted moving centre at a single instant. Grip slip during approach, gravity torque, continuous swept collision and contact physics are outside this calculation.

Follow the messages and frames
The navigation action accepts a goal, publishes feedback, supports cancellation and returns a result. The encoder node publishes wheel odometry. A landmark correction adjusts the transform from odometry to map; it leaves the recorded wheel distance intact. The quality of service (QoS) setting emulates reliable versus best-effort delivery. A reliable reader cannot use a best-effort writer. Local wheel control continues while messages for the external localisation subscriber are lost.
Map position equals the map-to-odometry translation plus odometry position. Each delivered landmark uses scalar measurement variance 0.0025 m² to reduce position uncertainty. Lost landmark messages, wheel slip and a wrong goal have different signatures. Inspect the recorded topics, physical endpoint and action result separately.


Built-in deterministic model. Localisation uses translation-only fusion and cardinal headings. Turning takes one 100 ms tick per quarter turn. A 0.15 m circular chassis checks physical clearance against occupied grid cells. MoveIt and the ROS 2 middleware do not execute inside this browser model.
