Model, exercise and acceptance
The built-in engine solves two planar revolute joints with analytical forward and inverse kinematics. Each joint moves at or below its configured rate, synchronised to arrive together. At 20 ms intervals the arm checks four substeps against a circular obstacle using 25 mm link radii. This sampled test can miss a narrow collision. The carton is stationary at pick and must be within 20 mm of the tool. The pallet accepts a release within 30 mm. Vacuum loss freezes the simulated grip for recovery. External dynamics, inertia, impact and safety certification are outside this calculation.
Begin with one normal cycle. For transfer, alter the base frame, move the carton and teach the new pick. Predict whether the targets are reachable. Remove vacuum before gripping, identify the first-out record and recover. Acceptance needs one matching identity on the pallet and one release event. For a timing investigation change only the joint rate, reset and compare cycle times.
