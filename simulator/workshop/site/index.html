04 / FIELD EXERCISE

Commission the mounting point

  1. Place a four-metre conveyor in a room and set its yaw to 90 degrees.
  2. Place a bracket at the belt entry. Seat it on the conveyor and move it 1.00 m along the conveyor axis.
  3. Compare the local longitudinal coordinate with the length of the belt. Return the measured position to the cell and check the detection time.
