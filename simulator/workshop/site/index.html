03 / FIT AND ALIGNMENT

Assembly relationships

Coordinates are global. X and Y locate the centre of the footprint. Z locates the underside. Yaw rotates around +Z, anticlockwise from +X. A parent groups the assembly tree.

04 / JOINTS

Mechanism and clearance

The selected asset is the moving child. Use the alignment reference as the fixed parent. Pivot X and Y are measured from the parent centre in its local frame. Revolute joints rotate about Z; prismatic joints travel along parent X. A fixed joint transmits its parent pose.

Supply 3 to 16 perimeter vertices in local length and width fractions from -0.5 to +0.5. An empty field uses the part rectangle. The footprint extrudes through the part height.

Author a joint sequence

 
Capture the first pose at zero, drive joints, then capture a later pose. Linear segments interpolate each joint coordinate before resolving its descendants. Hold keeps its original pose and jumps at the next key. The clearance check uses conservative speed bounds and midpoint subdivision. Results can be clear, collision or unresolved at the 1 ms or computation limit.
Intersection uses convex prismatic envelopes with 1 mm tolerance. Room and work-cell boundaries are excluded. A detected overlap can be an intended mounting interface. Check the selected geometry before changing it.
05 / FIELD EXERCISE
Commission the mounting point
  1. Place a four-metre conveyor in a room and set its yaw to 90 degrees.
  2. Place a bracket at the belt entry. Seat it on the conveyor and move it 1.00 m along the conveyor axis.
  3. Compare the local longitudinal coordinate with the length of the belt. Return the measured position to the cell and check the detection time.
