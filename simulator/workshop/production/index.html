Overall equipment effectiveness (OEE) multiplies availability, performance and quality. The full run begins at simulation time zero. Ratios include start-up and material residence time.

Historian and process variation
Source timestamps are simulation seconds. Samples are scheduled every 15 s while the historian is online. Display compression retains the first and last points. Statistical process control (SPC) uses uncompressed samples. Choose an approximately stationary baseline.


Fault duration Pareto
Occurrence and clearance come from the fault journal. Estimated mean time between failures (MTBF) uses fault-free observed seconds / occurrences for each cause. Mean time to repair (MTTR) uses completed repair seconds / completed repairs. Open incidents contribute to observed duration. Overlapping causes share clock time.


Lot transformation and recall
Start from finished pending and released inventory at this run boundary. Split an allocation, rework it with measured loss and recombine two parents. This separate mass ledger retains source holds. The reference run stays at its recorded state.


Author a plant process recipe
A registered revision drives the real reference plant material and temperature model. Try a 9 mm cut at 720 s freezer residence, then a 15 mm cut at 120 s. Predict the first packing time, yield and cold-chain result over the same 2,400 s window. Synthetic thermal lag uses 450 s × (cut width / 9 mm)². A short or thick cut can leave the core too warm for release.

Maintenance and food-safety capstones
Load Packaging bottleneck. Hold the line, isolate the packer, inspect and repair, release isolation, resume, then advance time and verify material flow. Compare preventive work before a predicted failure against a reactive work order. The original wear counter and retained inventory remain visible.
For an unsafe product investigation, load Foreign-body hold. Hold the line, identify exposed lots, record a mock recall, preserve affected shipment identities and reject compromised material.
