Find a missing signal
Start with the relay exercise. Measure S1.out against PS1.0V with the switch open, then closed. Measure the same points across K1.A1 and K1.A2. The coil controls a separate normally open contact. Check DI0.signal against its common. Remove the common wire, then compare the instrument reading with the input indication.
This DC nodal model uses one supply with 0.2 Ω internal resistance. Coils pick up at 18 V. A contact follows the previous sample. Inputs and lamps turn on above 12 V. A fuse opens on the sample after exceeding its rating. A disconnected floating network has no defined reference. Current and power use the configured load resistance. All work is virtual.
