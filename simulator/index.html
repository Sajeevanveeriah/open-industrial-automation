    Keyboard shortcuts

    Shortcuts apply when focus is outside a text field. Plant commands are never bound to single keys.

    Search workspaces, equipment, commands and scenarios
    Space
    Play or pause the simulation clock
    .
    Advance one simulated minute

    Advance ten simulated minutes
    [ ]
    Slower or faster playback
    T
    Switch light and dark theme
    ?

    Esc
    Close a dialog or the navigation drawer
